System Architecture

Transparent Geospatial AI Engineering

The GeoAI Outdoor Activity Assistant provides evidence-backed outdoor suitability ratings without relying on opaque black-box recommendations. Every score combines live atmospheric physics with real geographic venue proximity.

Scoring weighs precipitation probability (heavy penalty for rain in tennis, cricket, picnics), thermal comfort curves (ideal 18–24°C for high exertion cardio), wind resistance thresholds (critical for cycling and golf), and air quality index (WHO particulate thresholds).

We integrate directly with Open-Meteo's weather models (hourly temperature, relative humidity, precipitation, wind gusts) and Air Quality models (PM2.5, PM10, European AQI, NO2, O3).

The application queries OpenStreetMap nodes and ways tagging leisure=park, leisure=pitch, leisure=golf_course, leisure=track, and tourism=picnic_site within the specified radius around the coordinate center.
